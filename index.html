    Data sources and methodology

    The street map uses OpenStreetMap data via OpenFreeMap. Roads, buildings, addresses and points of interest depend on map coverage and zoom level.

    Opportunity scores, minimum investment estimates and the heatmap are simulated. The score weights are 45% demand, 35% competition gap and 20% accessibility. Budget filters the list by illustrative startup cost. Neighbourhood pins are approximate centres.
